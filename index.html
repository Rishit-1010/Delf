About Us

DELF Consulting Engineers (India) Pvt. Ltd. is a multi-disciplinary group of professionals providing complete Consultancy Services, based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.

It is an extension of DELF - South Africa as a Civil and Structural consultancy providing high quality, innovative and creative engineering services. Established in 2007 in India, with offering an integrated suite of services across all sectors such as, Residential buildings, Commercial buildings, Institutional building, Road infrastructures such as Highway, Bridges, Culverts, Industrial Structures including water & waste water treatments plants, industrial process plants, warehouses, silos, chimneys and equipment foundations, etc. DELF (India) is committed in providing consulting services through a team of top-notch professionals and the use of State of the art techniques for Strategy, Feasibilities study, Planning, Designing, Costing, Project Management, Quality Control & Quality Assurance and Pre bid services.

Our expertise has carved a niche and extended our projects regionally as well as nationally. Through our vast experience and expertise, we ensure our clients are delivered solutions adhering to their needs which are compliant to statutory, local as well as international norms. DELF (India) has also endeavored to bring in innovation to its projects. To empower these work standards, we have setup state-of-the-art infrastructure. We have internationally acclaimed and accepted softwares for our core design and drafting department.
